# Tips and shortcuts

- **Ctrl+S** saves sites, antennas, plots and links.
- **F1** opens this guide.
- **Right-click the map** to start a new site there.
- **Double-click** a site or a plot in its list to zoom the map to it.
- Click a pin on the map to select that site; its plot is selected on the Coverage tab.
- Hold the pointer over a setting to see what it does.

## Units

- dBm is power relative to 1 mW. −107 dBm ≈ 1 µV, −113 dBm ≈ 0.5 µV and −119 dBm ≈ 0.25 µV (pd, 50 Ω).
- dBi is antenna gain over an isotropic radiator; a half-wave dipole is 2.15 dBi.
- EIRP is the transmitter power minus feeder loss plus antenna gain; ERP is EIRP − 2.15 dB.

## Good practice

- Plan with **90 % of spots** and the right **surroundings**; the median (50 %) is too kind for planning.
- Check a promising site with the **Horizon** tab first, then plot coverage, then confirm key paths with **Links**.
- Higher terrain detail is slower and downloads more, but helps in hilly country and for short links.
